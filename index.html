behind the manuscript

What began as a
personal grimoire

What started as my own private notes spiralled into something far larger: a meticulously researched, cross-referenced database that reflects the relationships between every aspect of magickal practice. I'm neurodivergent, and this work became a deep special interest. I needed to see how every part of the craft connects in order to truly understand it.

Every entry is drawn from reputable published sources: books by established scholars and practitioners in witchcraft, folklore, and occult studies. The depth of traditional research, made searchable for a modern practice.

My personal practice is rooted in plant work, herbs, candles, tarot, runes, and crystals, with a strong focus on protection magick. Every entry in the Manuscript reflects something I have personally researched and, in most cases, used. My husband handles the technical architecture. I handle the research. Together we built the resource we couldn't find anywhere else.
