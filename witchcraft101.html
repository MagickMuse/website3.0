Witchcraft 101 is a self-paced course built from the ground up using published sources and hands-on practice you do yourself. Designed by a practicing teacher and lifelong witch, it gives you a real foundation in the craft.

Seven units. Each one includes readings, key terms, practical application, a discussion prompt, and homework. By the end, you will have cast your first spell with real understanding behind it.

The Curriculum

What you'll learn

  1. 01

    What Is Witchcraft

    What witchcraft actually is, where it comes from, and how to think about it clearly before you begin your practice.

  2. 02

    Tools and Altars

    What you actually need to start, what is optional, and how to build an altar that works for your space and life.

  3. 03

    Correspondences

    The system of symbolic relationships that underpins almost all spellwork. How correspondences function and how to use them with intention.

  4. 04

    Spiritual Hygiene

    Cleansing, grounding, shielding, and warding. The protective practices every practitioner needs before moving into spellwork.

  5. 05

    Shadow Work

    What shadow work is, why it matters for a serious practice, and how to begin without overwhelming yourself.

  6. 06

    Spell Preparation

    Research, timing, components, and intention. Everything that goes into a spell before you cast it.

  7. 07

    Casting a Spell

    The act itself, from opening to closing. You will cast your first spell with full understanding of what you are doing and why.

Every unit includes readings • key terms • practical application • a discussion prompt • homework
